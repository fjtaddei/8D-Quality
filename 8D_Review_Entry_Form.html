D-Step Scoring

ℹ Score each discipline based on evidence quality — not template completion. Use the rubric below each step. Do not score from memory; read the 8D report while scoring.
D1 Team Formation ×1
— ▼
0 — No team or owner identified.
1 — Owner named but missing key functions or unclear accountability.
2 — Appropriate owner and most functions engaged.
3 — Cross-functional team aligned to issue source, clear accountable owner, defined cadence. Evidence: team list, function map.
⚠ Low score — verify owner accountability and cross-functional coverage before accepting.
D2 Problem Description ×1.5
—
0 — Problem not defined.
1 — Narrative only; weak quantification or scope.
2 — Problem defined with basic data and scope.
3 — Clear, quantified, segmented IS/IS-NOT statement. What is and is not affected, with defect rate, location, timing, serial/batch scope.
⚠ Weak problem definition — garbage in, garbage out on root cause. D4 score is unreliable without this.
D3 Interim Containment ×1
—
0 — Containment absent.
1 — Containment launched but incomplete or not verified.
2 — Containment implemented with basic coverage.
3 — Fast, complete, traceable, and verified across WIP/FG/shipments. Evidence: stock checks, suspect inventory status, verification results.
⚠ Containment weakness — check whether WIP, finished goods, and in-transit shipments were all covered and verified.
D4a Occurrence Root Cause ×2
—
Why the defect was produced.
0 — No root cause or unsupported guess.
1 — Likely cause stated but not validated; assumption only.
2 — Root cause supported with some evidence.
3 — Validated causal chain with evidence or trials — team demonstrated ability to turn the defect on and off. Evidence: 5 Why, fishbone, test data, process comparisons.
🚨 Critical gap — unvalidated root cause means the corrective action is guesswork. D5–D7 scores are unreliable.
D4b Escape Point ×2
—
Why the detection system failed to catch it.
0 — Escape point not addressed.
1 — Escape mentioned but not analysed.
2 — Escape point identified with partial evidence.
3 — Verified escape point with evidence — explains exactly where and why the control failed. Evidence: control plan review, inspection records, detection gap analysis.
🚨 Escape point missing — even a correct occurrence root cause fix will not prevent future customer escapes without addressing the detection failure.
D5 Action Selection ×1
—
0 — No permanent actions defined.
1 — Actions generic or administrative; weak linkage to cause.
2 — Actions generally linked to cause.
3 — Actions clearly linked to both occurrence and escape causes, prioritised by risk and sustainability. Side effects verified. Evidence: action list, risk rationale, poka-yoke documentation.
⚠ Verify that actions address both the occurrence root cause (D4a) and the escape point (D4b), not just one.
D6 Validation ×1.5
—
0 — Not implemented or not validated.
1 — Implemented but late or without effectiveness evidence.
2 — Implemented and basic effectiveness shown.
3 — Implemented on time with objective before/after data confirming effectiveness and residual risk reviewed. Evidence: PPM trend, scrap trend, audit results, trial data.
⚠ No data, no proof. A statement that the action was implemented is not validation — before/after evidence is required.
D7 Prevent Recurrence ×2
— CAPPED
0 — No prevention update.
1 — Local fix only; standards not updated.
2 — Some system updates made.
3 — PFMEA, Control Plan, Work Instructions, training, and audit system updated AND deployed to similar processes or sites. Evidence: updated PFMEA, control plan, standard work, training records, layered audit records.
⚠ D7 is where most organisations fail — they fix the one line and never deploy horizontally. Check for evidence of system-level updates, not just local fixes.
🚨 Recurrence confirmed — D7 is automatically capped at 1. Prevention failed by definition.
D8 Closure Discipline ×1
—
0 — Case not formally closed.
1 — Closure incomplete or poorly documented.
2 — Closure complete with basic lessons learned.
3 — Closure disciplined; lessons captured and shared; customer accepted where required. Evidence: closure approval, VOC closure confirmation, lessons learned record.
⚠ Incomplete closure means lessons learned are not captured and the case is not formally resolved from a customer perspective.
